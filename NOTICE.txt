StarTag – Texas SPV for Marketplace

StarTag is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles
(TxDMV) or Meta Platforms, Inc. Facebook and Marketplace are trademarks of Meta Platforms, Inc.;
the names only describe the pages the extension works on. SPV values come from TxDMV's Standard
Presumptive Value calculator (tools.txdmv.gov) at the time of the lookup; none are shipped with the
extension.

Data

- data/makes.json and data/index/ are built from NHTSA's Product Information Catalog and Vehicle
  Listing (vPIC), a work of the U.S. government in the public domain, from the vpic.lite.db file
  of the @cardog/corgi package (https://github.com/cardog-ai/corgi, license below).
- The real VIN prefixes in data/index/ (VIN positions 1-8 and 10, with counts) come from NHTSA's
  consumer complaints file (https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/ffdd/cmpl/FLAT_CMPL.zip), a work of the
  U.S. government in the public domain.
